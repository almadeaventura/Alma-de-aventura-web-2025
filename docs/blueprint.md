# **App Name**: AlmAventura

## Core Features:

- Adventure Carousel: Showcase featured adventures with a captivating video or image carousel. Each slide includes a brief description and a call to action to learn more or book directly.
- WhatsApp Integration: Implement a prominent WhatsApp contact form that allows potential adventurers to quickly reach out for inquiries and bookings.
- Curated Adventure Listings: Display a selection of adventures, each including an image, a brief description, share and play buttons, a 'Ver Más' link, and a booking option.
- Customer Testimonials: Feature customer testimonials in a horizontal scrolling section to build trust and credibility.
- Footer with Quick Links and Social Media: A comprehensive footer containing quick links to important pages (About Us, Tours, FAQ, Contact), social media links, and contact information.
- Booking Form: Integrate a simple form for collecting user's name and WhatsApp number, triggering direct contact for booking arrangements.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Bright yellow (#FFD100) to convey energy and excitement.
- Background: Light gray (#F0F2F5) for a clean, modern look.
- Accent color: Steel blue/silver (#8A99A6) for secondary text and elements.
- Headline font: 'Plus Jakarta Sans' (sans-serif) for a modern and bold feel.
- Body font: 'Plus Jakarta Sans' (sans-serif) for clear and readable text.
- Use Material Symbols Outlined for a consistent and modern look.
- Use a grid-based layout for the adventure listings to ensure a structured and responsive design.
